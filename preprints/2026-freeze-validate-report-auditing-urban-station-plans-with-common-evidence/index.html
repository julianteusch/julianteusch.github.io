Freeze, Validate, Report: Auditing Urban Station Plans with Common Evidence

Julian Teusch, Oliver Keszöcze

arXiv

Abstract

Freeze–Validate–Report compares urban station plans under a shared evaluation contract rather than plan-dependent inputs. Seven generators are assessed in Porto and Chicago using common validation evidence and a declared selection rule. Input-sensitivity checks and reproducible decision logs support auditability within that contract, not guarantees of stable deployment performance. Accepted at UrbanAI 2026, co-located with ACM SIGSPATIAL.
